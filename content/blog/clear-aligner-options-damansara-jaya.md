## Why Clear Aligners Are Popular

Clear aligners have become one of the most requested orthodontic treatments in recent years. Unlike traditional braces, which use metal brackets and wires, clear aligners consist of a series of transparent plastic trays that gradually move your teeth into the desired position.

The main benefits that attract patients include:

- **Discreet appearance** — the transparent material makes aligners much less visible than metal braces
- **Removability** — you can take them out for meals and oral care
- **Fewer food restrictions** — since aligners are removed for eating, you can continue enjoying the foods you like
- **Comfort** — smooth plastic means less irritation to the inside of the mouth
- **Fewer dental visits** — aligner changes happen at home, with scheduled check-ups to monitor progress

### Invisalign

Invisalign is one of the most recognised clear aligner brands worldwide. It uses a proprietary SmartTrack material and ClinCheck software to plan and track tooth movement. Invisalign can treat a broad range of cases, from mild crowding to more complex orthodontic issues.

Invisalign First is also available for children who are still in the mixed dentition stage, making it an option for younger patients who need early orthodontic intervention.

### Angel Aligner

Angel Aligner is another clear aligner system with its own treatment planning approach. Like Invisalign, it uses a series of custom-made aligners to gradually move teeth. The system is known for its affordability while maintaining quality outcomes.

### ClearSmile

ClearSmile offers an alternative aligner system that also uses a series of removable trays. It provides another option for patients seeking clear aligner treatment.

## Who Is a Good Candidate for Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are suitable for many adults and teenagers who want to straighten their teeth. You may be a good candidate if you have:

- Mild to moderate crowding or spacing
- Minor bite issues
- Relapsed teeth (teeth that have shifted after previous orthodontic treatment)
- A desire for a less visible treatment option

More complex cases may still be treatable with clear aligners, depending on the specific situation. Your dentist will assess your teeth and bite to determine whether clear aligners are appropriate for your needs.

## What to Expect During Treatment

### Initial Consultation

The process starts with a consultation. Your dentist will examine your teeth, discuss your goals, and take records such as photos, X-rays, or a 3D scan of your mouth. They will then recommend the most appropriate treatment approach.

### Treatment Planning

Once a system is selected, a digital treatment plan is created. This plan shows how your teeth are expected to move from start to finish. You will be able to see an estimate of what your final result may look like.

### Wearing Your Aligners

You will receive a series of aligner trays, each worn for about one to two weeks before moving to the next in the sequence. Aligners should be worn for twenty to twenty-two hours per day. You remove them only for eating, drinking (anything other than water), brushing, and flossing.

### Check-Up Appointments

Regular check-up appointments are scheduled to monitor progress. These are usually every six to eight weeks, though the frequency depends on your treatment plan.

### Retention

After the active treatment phase, you will need to wear retainers to keep your teeth in their new position. Your dentist will advise on the appropriate retention plan.
