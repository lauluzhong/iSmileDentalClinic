### Understanding the Materials

Composite veneers are skillfully sculpted directly onto your teeth in a single visit. They are highly versatile and easily reparable if ever needed. Porcelain veneers are custom-crafted in a specialized lab, offering exceptional strength and a beautiful, glass-like translucency that mimics natural enamel perfectly.

### Durability and Long-Term Beauty

Porcelain is highly resistant to staining from coffee or tea and typically lasts 10-15 years with proper care. Composite is more porous and may require periodic polishing to maintain its luster, usually lasting around 5-7 years.

### Preserving Your Natural Teeth

Composite often requires very little removal of your natural tooth structure, making it a conservative choice. Porcelain may require a minimal amount of reshaping to ensuring the veneers look natural and never bulky.

### The Best Choice for You

For younger patients or minor corrections, composite is often the ideal conservative choice. For a complete, long-lasting transformation, porcelain remains the gold standard in cosmetic dentistry.
