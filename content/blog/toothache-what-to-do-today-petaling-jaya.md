A toothache rarely waits for a good time. Here is what you can do today, the signs that mean you should go to a hospital, and how to get seen at our clinic in Damansara Jaya.

## What helps today

<ul style="list-style:none;padding-left:0">
<li>✅ Rinse gently with warm salt water.</li>
<li>✅ Chew on the other side and keep to soft, lukewarm food.</li>
<li>✅ Hold a cold pack on the outside of your cheek, 15 minutes at a time.</li>
<li>✅ Ask a pharmacist which pain relief is safe for you, and follow the label.</li>
<li>✅ Keep your head a little raised when you lie down.</li>
</ul>

These steps take the edge off. They do not treat the cause, so plan to see a dentist even if the pain fades.

## What to avoid

<ul style="list-style:none;padding-left:0">
<li>❌ Putting a painkiller tablet against the gum. It can burn the gum.</li>
<li>❌ Very hot, very cold or sweet food and drinks on the sore side.</li>
<li>❌ Poking at the tooth or a broken edge.</li>
<li>❌ Waiting weeks because the pain comes and goes.</li>
</ul>

## When to go to a hospital instead

Go to a hospital emergency department straight away if you have:

<ul style="list-style:none;padding-left:0">
<li>❌ Swelling in your face, jaw or neck that is getting bigger quickly</li>
<li>❌ A fever with the toothache</li>
<li>❌ Trouble swallowing, breathing or opening your mouth</li>
</ul>

These can be signs of an infection that is spreading. A hospital can treat that at any hour.

## Common reasons a tooth hurts

<ul style="list-style:none;padding-left:0">
<li>➖ <strong>A cavity</strong> that has reached deep into the tooth</li>
<li>➖ <strong>A cracked tooth or a lost filling</strong>, often painful when you bite</li>
<li>➖ <strong>An infection</strong> at the root, sometimes with a bad taste or a bump on the gum</li>
<li>➖ <strong>A wisdom tooth</strong> coming through at the back</li>
<li>➖ <strong>Sore gums</strong> around a tooth</li>
</ul>

Only an examination can tell which one it is. An X-ray often helps.

## What happens after the first visit

The first visit is about relief and a clear answer. If the area is swollen, the dentist may give medicine and ask you to come back once the swelling goes down, because treatment is often easier and more comfortable then.

The follow-up depends on the cause. It may be a filling, [root canal treatment](/services/protect/root-canal) to save the tooth, or removing a tooth that cannot be saved. The dentist explains the options before anything is done.

If toothaches keep coming back, regular check-ups catch most problems while they are still small. Our [general and preventive care](/services/protect) covers check-ups, cleaning, fillings and root canal treatment for the whole family.
