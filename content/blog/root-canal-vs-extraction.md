### Choosing the Best Path for Your Smile

The words "root canal" often spark fear, leading many patients to ask, "Can't you just pull it?" While extraction might seem like the quicker, simpler fix in the moment, it often leads to more complex issues down the road.

Our goal is always to help you keep your natural teeth whenever possible. Understanding the trade-offs between these two procedures is key to making a decision you won't regret later.

![Root Canal vs Extraction comparison](/images/blog/root_vs_extraction.png)

### Saving Your Natural Tooth

A root canal clears out infection while preserving the tooth structure. This means you keep your natural bite, alignment, and jawbone density. No replacement tooth feels or functions exactly like your own natural teeth.

### The "One and Done" Myth of Extraction

Pulling a tooth seems final, but it's often just the beginning of a new dental journey. When a tooth is missing, surrounding teeth can shift, and the jawbone begins to shrink. To restore function and prevent further issues, you may eventually need a bridge or dental implant.

### Maintaining Jawbone Health

Your teeth stimulate your jawbone. When one is lost, that stimulation stops, which can lead to bone loss over time. Saving the tooth with a root canal maintains this vital relationship and keeps your facial structure supported.

![Dentist explaining dental X-ray](/images/blog/dentist_xray.png)

### Plan for Long-Term Oral Health

Preserving your natural dental structure is usually the best investment for long-term health. It prevents the domino effect of shifting teeth that can complicate future treatment needs.

### When Early Action Matters

If you are experiencing throbbing pain, sensitivity to heat, or swelling, don't wait. Early intervention often saves the tooth and avoids more invasive procedures later.
