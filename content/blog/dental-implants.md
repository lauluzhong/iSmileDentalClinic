# Dental Implants in Malaysia: What You Need to Know

Losing a tooth can feel overwhelming. Whether it's from an accident, decay, or gum disease, the gap left behind affects more than just your smile.

Dental implants provide a permanent foundation for crowns that look, feel, and function like your natural teeth.

## Why Choose Dental Implants?

- Preserve jawbone health
- Don't require reducing healthy neighbouring teeth
- Look and feel like natural teeth
- Long-lasting solution for missing teeth

## What to Expect

1. **Consultation** — We'll assess your situation and plan your treatment
2. **Placement** — The implant is placed under local anesthesia
3. **Healing** — Time for the implant to integrate with your jawbone
4. **Crown Attachment** — Your custom crown is attached
