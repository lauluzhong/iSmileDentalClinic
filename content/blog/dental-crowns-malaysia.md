# Dental Crowns in Malaysia: What You Need to Know

If your dentist has mentioned you need a dental crown, you probably have questions. Here is what you need to know about getting a crown in Malaysia.

## What Is a Dental Crown?

A dental crown is a custom-made cap that covers a damaged or weakened tooth. It restores the tooth's shape, size, strength, and appearance. Crowns are typically recommended when a tooth has been significantly weakened by decay, a large filling, or a root canal treatment.

## Why Might You Need a Crown?

- **Large cavity** too big for a standard filling
- **Cracked or worn tooth** from grinding or injury
- **After a root canal** to protect the treated tooth
- **Misshapen or discoloured tooth** for cosmetic improvement
- **To anchor a dental bridge** in place

## Types of Dental Crowns Available in Malaysia

| Type | Material | Pros | Cons |
|------|----------|------|------|
| **Porcelain** | Ceramic | Natural appearance, metal-free | Less durable than other options |
| **Porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM)** | Metal + porcelain | Strong and aesthetic | Dark line may show at gum |
| **Full metal** | Gold or metal alloy | Most durable | Not aesthetic for front teeth |
| **Zirconia** | Zirconium dioxide | Strong, natural-looking, metal-free | Higher cost |

## The Procedure

Getting a dental crown typically requires two visits.

**First visit:** The tooth is prepared by removing any decay and shaping it to fit the crown. An impression is taken and sent to a dental lab. A temporary crown is placed.

**Second visit:** The permanent crown is fitted, adjusted, and cemented into place.

Some clinics offer same-day crowns using CEREC or similar systems.

## Is a Crown Right for You?

If you have a damaged or weakened tooth, a dental crown may be the key solution to restore your smile and protect your oral health. Speak with your dentist to discuss your options and find the right treatment plan for your needs.
